How booking works

Most bookings are confirmed in under 15 minutes.

  1. Message or call us

    WhatsApp +91 95295 00048, or call +91 95295 00048 or +91 89991 33165. Tell us the car, your dates and where you're landing or staying.

  2. Send your licence and ID

    A photo of each is enough to confirm. You show the originals at handover.

  3. Confirm your booking

    We send the full quote on WhatsApp. A small advance by UPI holds the car.

  4. Get the keys where you are

    Airport arrivals, railway station, hotel gate. We walk round the car with you and note any marks.

  5. Drop it off and get your deposit back

    Return it at the airport or your hotel with the same fuel level. Deposits come back within 24 hours.

Questions people ask

Do you deliver self-drive cars in Panjim?

Yes. We deliver to Panjim city, Miramar, Dona Paula, Old Goa, Porvorim. Free delivery in and around Panjim.

What documents do I need to rent a self-drive car in Goa?

A driving licence you've held for at least one year, and an ID such as Aadhaar or a passport. Send photos on WhatsApp when you book and show the originals at handover.

Is there a security deposit?

Yes. The deposit depends on the car and is refunded in full when you return it without new damage, usually within 24 hours. We confirm the amount on WhatsApp when you book.

Can you deliver the car to Mopa or Dabolim airport?

Yes. Tell us your flight number and we will be at arrivals with the car, even if your flight is late.

Is there a kilometre limit?

Rentals include 300 km per day. If you plan a longer trip, tell us when you book and we will suggest the best option.

Can I drive the car outside Goa?

Ask us before you book. Trips to Gokarna or Kolhapur are usually possible with a state permit.

Are your cars legal self-drive rentals?

Yes. Every CarGoa car is registered for self-drive hire and carries the black plate with yellow letters that the law requires. Avoid private white-plate cars offered for rent: they aren't insured for it.
